# v0.1.8

Verifier fixes from one external report and its review, and the rule code of the live game since
v0.1.7: the Market sale tax, the queue Joust pot, the live Squire quote, the Mint 1k summon of the
new war balance, the Farm hunger rule and the Pale Vigil reward seed. Public source for audit,
under BUSL-1.1.

## Deployed program

The on-chain program is unchanged from v0.1.4: same program id, same source and the same
verifiable build hashes listed in [v0.1.4](https://github.com/Crypto-of-Thrones/core/blob/v0.1.4/docs/releases/v0.1.4.md).
No file under `programs/` changed.

## Changes

### Verifier: external report

This issue was reported by an independent security researcher. Thank you.

- **Market and live hunt entries hold their balance change.** `marketSale`, `marketBuy` and
  `huntLive` entries used to be checked on their `refs` only, so any balance change passed. They
  are now held to the exact pockets the game books (`STATIC_PAID` in `web/js/ledgerverify.js`):
  a sale credits the Hunt chest with the price minus the tax, a purchase debits the price from the
  pocket it was paid from, and a live hunt tick credits exactly its chest, war and war balance
  amounts. A mismatch fails as `bad` (`amount`).
- **No draw on those entries.** A `marketSale`, `marketBuy` or `huntLive` entry that carries a draw
  fails as `bad` (`draw`). Before, such an entry was read as a paid act and could stay `wait`.
- **Mainnet pockets.** With `--mainnet` a sale pays only the Hunt chest, and a wallet purchase is
  accepted only as an on-chain order with no balance change. The test wallet pocket fails there.
- **Cancelled match reveal.** A cancelled Duel or Joust reveal must move no balance at all, not
  only leave the Hunt chest untouched.

### Verifier: rules of the live game

- **Market sale tax by time.** From 2026-11-27 00:00 UTC (`MARKET_TAX10_FROM`) the Market sale tax
  is 10% of the price in one team row; before it, 12% in the previous two rows (6% burn and 6%
  Treasury). The tax recorded on a wallet purchase (`roiTax`) is checked at the rate of the entry's
  own time, and an undated wallet purchase fails. The old "10% or 12%" fallback is gone.
- **Queue Joust pot.** When both lords of a queue match paid the entry, the winner may also take
  the foe's win (`refs.foeWin`), capped by `joustWinCap`, and only with a non-empty `refs.foeEntry`
  naming the foe's join. A `foeWin` on a free entry, without `foeEntry` or above the cap fails.
- **Live Squire quote.** A Squire act that records `refs.quote` (and no `refs.usd`) is priced at
  `quote.cot`, or at the page promotion price when the act names a promotion whose full price is
  `quote.cot`. Older entries keep the `refs.usd` check.
- **Mint 1k on the new war balance.** A summon paid from the new war balance (`war: 'new'`) is
  priced at the Mint 1k price; one paid from the old war balance keeps the previous price.
- **Horse breeding reveal.** The `horseBreed` reveal rule checks the foal's rarity, generation and
  sex (`foalSex` in `web/js/horses.js`, from the draw). Horse breeding is not open in the game.

### Rules

- `web/js/marketrules.js`: `MARKET_TAX10_FROM`, `taxPctAt`; `taxOf` and `marketFee` take the time.
  A horse listing has a flat price floor (`MARKET.horseFloor`).
- `web/js/data.js`: Market tax 10% to the team (`ECONOMY.market.teamPct`); Farm food fields
  `barHours` and `graceUntil`; `summonPrice` takes the war balance; horse breeding constants.
- `web/js/homestead.js`: one meal keeps a mercenary fed for `barHours` hours (`fedUntil`), with an
  opening grace (`graceUntil`); `restFedSec`, `hungerLeft`, `starvingAt`.
- `web/js/huntengine.js`, `web/js/huntsim.js`: a hunt leg recorded with `hungerHunt` replays the
  food rule, so a hungry mercenary rests instead of hunting. Legs recorded without it replay as
  before. The Squire farm mode is read from the Squire being owned and on.
- `web/js/horses.js`: horse sex, generation and Stable pairs (`breedFields`, `foalSex`).
- `web/js/passrules.js`: a pass owner can claim the boat reward at any level (`EARLY_KINDS`).
- `web/js/raidrules.js`: the Pale Vigil reward seed is 168,000 base.
- `web/js/fusion.js`, `web/js/gear.js`: helper changes with no rule change (`pickPool`,
  `wornByTest`).
- `tools/replay-hunt-test.mjs`: test for the hunger rule of a hunt leg.
