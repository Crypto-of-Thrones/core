# v0.1.5

Verifier fixes and the game rules of the live game. Public source for audit, under BUSL-1.1.

## Deployed program

The on-chain program is unchanged from v0.1.4: same program id, same source and the same
verifiable build hashes listed in [v0.1.4](v0.1.4.md).

## Changes

### Verifier

- **Anchor cache.** The cache of on-chain anchor checks is keyed by the whole proof (epoch,
  Merkle root, leaf count, previous hash, hash, anchor mode, status and signature), not by the
  epoch only. A second proof of the same hour can no longer reuse the check of the first one.
- **Duel replay.** A Duel result is replayed from the published fight log
  (`<base>/duel/<match>/log`): winner, end, fighters, log and match must all match the entry.
  A Duel with no readable log is reported as `SIG` (signature only), never `PASS`.
- **Draw rebind bounds.** A rebind of a draw to a new round is accepted only from the single
  speed-up of the same send, written after the send and before both rounds existed. More than
  one rebind for a send fails, and the reveal must read the rebound round. A rebind on a masked
  row counts toward that limit but never supports a `PASS`.
- **Public view.** Rows the public view redacts or shortens are reported as hidden and never
  verified; a masked row without its mask fails. Account ids in the public view are keyed
  public audit ids. Reads retry on HTTP 503 with `Retry-After`.

### Rules

- `web/js/balance.js`: lower withdrawal minimum.
- `web/js/data.js`: referral, invite and free spin thresholds rescaled to the token prices.
- `web/js/duelsim.js`: v2 Duel record reader and the fight log replay used by the verifier.
- `web/js/expedition.js`, `web/js/gear.js`: second and third Dungeon and voyage slot need the
  Battle Pass.
- `web/js/fusion.js`, `web/js/huntsim.js`, `web/js/huntengine.js`, `web/js/marketrules.js`: new
  mint tier for new mercenaries; older mercenaries move to it on a fixed date set in the rules.
- `web/js/homestead.js`: mercenaries replace the farm workers, with a rarity bonus.
- `web/js/hunt.js`: ranged mercenaries keep a stand-off and shoot; foes move between duel posts.
- `web/js/huntengine.js`: Squire sends count for the hunt mission, without changing the pay.
- `web/js/invite.js`: active vassal threshold and milestones rescaled.
- `web/js/ladderrules.js`: the Slayer and Waves ladders are gated on the first spend.
- `web/js/lottery.js`: banned realms stay out of prize draws.
- `web/js/net.js`: public strings mask embedded account ids.
- `web/js/passrules.js`: daily raid limit with and without the Battle Pass.
- `web/js/pvpark.js`: PvP Ark rule module, imported by the verifier.
- `web/js/pvprules.js`: Protect lock after the PvP opening and no new-realm safety past it.
- `web/js/trial.js`: free spin gate reads the tokens invested.
- `web/js/worldboss.js`: room share codes drawn at random.

### Repository

- The public guard drops a rule that only applied to the private repository.
